Every wallet, in plain sight

Rank addresses by realized PnL on closed round-trips. Open one to see its tokens, its hold times and the win/loss run behind the number, with the win rate set against the range a coin-flipper with the same number of closes lands in. Holdings that arrived by transfer, issuance or bridge have no cost basis here and are not scored — about 72.7% of volume in the current seven-day window.

Frequently asked

Every figure here comes from the swap tape, and only closed round-trips are scored. The board and every address on it are open to read without an account.

01What is Peapod?

A read-only view of Robinhood Chain. Every address that traded in the window is ranked by realized PnL on the round-trips it closed, each one searchable, with a page per token carrying its price, 24-hour volume, price series and recent trades from the same tape.

02Do I need a wallet to use it?

No, and there is no way to connect one. Every page is open to read without an account, and nothing here signs in, holds a key or touches a balance.

03Does Peapod hold my funds?

No. Nothing here places, copies or simulates a trade, so there is nothing to hold — the copy-setup screens show exactly what a request would look like and say plainly which endpoint is not built.

04Which assets are covered?

Tokenized equities and memecoins traded on Robinhood Chain, including tokens launched on Pons. Spot only — no perps, no leverage, no funding, no liquidation.

05What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no plan to buy, no account to make and no paid tier; the board, the token pages and every address are free to read.
