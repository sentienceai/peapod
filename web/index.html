Frequently asked

Every figure above comes straight from what the chain shows — the leaderboard and every wallet on it are open to look through yourself.

01What is Peapod?

An under-the-hood view of Robinhood Chain: every wallet, trade and token, ranked by realized PnL on closed round-trips and searchable — so you can see who is actually making money, and copy them once copy trading is live.

02Do I need a wallet to use it?

No. The leaderboard and every wallet profile are open to everyone. You’d only connect a wallet to copy or automate a strategy, and that step isn’t live yet.

03Does Peapod hold my funds?

No custody model exists yet, because nothing here places, copies or simulates a trade — the copy-setup screens show exactly what a request would look like and say plainly which endpoint isn’t built.

04Which assets are covered?

Tokenized equities and memecoins traded on Robinhood Chain, including tokens launched on Pons. Spot only — no perps, no leverage, no funding, no liquidation.

05What does it cost?

Pricing hasn’t launched. The leaderboard and every wallet profile are free to browse today.
